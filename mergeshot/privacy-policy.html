Privacy Policy

Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 Developer: Sagar Soneji Package ID: com.sagar.mergeshot.numberpuzzle2048
Summary: Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle is designed to respect your privacy. Your game progress, high scores, coins, missions, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not require account sign-in, we do not operate user-tracking backend servers, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

The Application does not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address to play.

All gameplay state and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private application storage (SharedPreferences):

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Gameplay State & Scores Saves your active board, current level, best scores, coin balance, and daily streak progress. On-device local storage only
App Preferences Stores your selected theme (Slate / Paper), audio volume, haptics toggle, and Smooth Mode (refresh rate) setting. On-device local storage only
Opt-In Reminder Schedule Stores whether daily puzzle reminders are enabled and your chosen reminder hour. On-device local storage only
Gameplay Metrics Records local session counters (games played, merges, streak milestones, and ad/purchase prompts) to power weekly missions and in-game milestones. On-device local storage only

2. Device Permissions & System Checks

The Application declares only the minimal permissions needed for core gameplay and optional features:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during tile launches, merges, and danger warnings. You can disable haptics anytime in the Pause menu.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Strictly optional (opt-in). If you choose to enable Daily Puzzle reminders, the app schedules local on-device notifications (at most once per day) via Android AlarmManager. No push notification tokens or remote messaging servers are used.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used when loading web fonts, checking optional remote feature/ad configuration, opening external links (such as this Privacy Policy), or connecting with Google Play services.
  • Power & Display State: The app queries Android's local PowerManager and BatteryManager status strictly on-device to automatically cap animations at 60Hz when battery saver is active or battery level is below 15%. No battery or hardware data leaves your device.

3. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. To keep Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle free to play, the Application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) by Google LLC to serve optional rewarded advertisements (such as free hints, continues, or coin bonuses) and infrequent level-transition interstitial ads (disabled if you purchase "Remove Ads").

When ads are served, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction signals:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and device/app instance identifiers.
  • Approximate Location: General region or country derived from your IP address for ad relevance and fraud prevention.
  • Ad & App Interactions: Ad impressions, clicks, video completion events, and frequency-capping state.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and SDK performance metrics.

Consent & Ad Controls (GDPR / EEA / UK / Regional Privacy): Where required by law, we present a consent dialog via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before loading personalized ads, allowing you to accept, manage preferences, or opt for non-personalized ads. You can also manage or reset your Advertising ID anytime:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Google Privacy & Terms: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services & Google Privacy Policy.
