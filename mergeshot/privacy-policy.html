Privacy Policy

Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 Developer: Sagar Soneji Package ID: com.sagar.mergeshot.numberpuzzle2048
Summary: Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle is designed to respect your privacy. Your game progress, high scores, coins, missions, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not require account sign-in, we do not operate user-tracking backend servers, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

The Application does not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address to play.

All gameplay state and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private application storage (SharedPreferences):

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Gameplay State & Scores Saves your active board, current level, best scores, coin balance, and daily streak progress. On-device local storage only
App Preferences Stores your selected theme (Slate / Paper), audio volume, haptics toggle, and Smooth Mode (refresh rate) setting. On-device local storage only
Opt-In Reminder Schedule Stores whether daily puzzle reminders are enabled and your chosen reminder hour. On-device local storage only
Gameplay Metrics Records local session counters (games played, merges, streak milestones, and ad/purchase prompts) to power weekly missions and in-game milestones. On-device local storage only

2. Device Permissions & System Checks

The Application declares only the minimal permissions needed for core gameplay and optional features:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during tile launches, merges, and danger warnings. You can disable haptics anytime in the Pause menu.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Strictly optional (opt-in). If you choose to enable Daily Puzzle reminders, the app schedules local on-device notifications (at most once per day) via Android AlarmManager. No push notification tokens or remote messaging servers are used.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used when loading web fonts, checking optional remote feature/ad configuration, opening external links (such as this Privacy Policy), or connecting with Google Play services.
  • Power & Display State: The app queries Android's local PowerManager and BatteryManager status strictly on-device to automatically cap animations at 60Hz when battery saver is active or battery level is below 15%. No battery or hardware data leaves your device.

3. Advertising, In-App Purchases & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

Depending on your app version and regional configuration, the Application may integrate the following trusted Google Play platform services:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing: Handles optional in-app purchases (such as the one-time "Remove Ads + Support" upgrade) securely through Google Play. Payment details are processed solely by Google LLC; the developer never receives your credit card or billing details.
  • Advertising Services (Rewarded & Interstitial Ads): Optional rewarded ads (for free hints, continues, or coin bonuses) and infrequent level-transition ads may be served via Google Play / Google Mobile Ads SDKs. Where enabled, Google may process device advertising identifiers (AAID), IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or limit your Google Advertising ID anytime on Android via: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

4. Data Sharing Disclosure

Because the developer does not maintain remote user databases, we do not collect, access, or share personal profiles. Any technical diagnostics, crash logs, or purchase/ad signals processed automatically through the Google Play Store or Google Play Services are governed by Google's data processing terms and shared only with Google LLC for app delivery, fraud prevention, billing verification, and ad serving (where applicable).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data Deletion: All local game data, scores, and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data in Android System Settings or uninstall the Application. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes its private local storage directory.
  • Notification Opt-Out: You can disable reminders anytime inside the app or via Android System Notification Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Merge Shot: 2048 Number Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Security

All local game records are protected within the operating system's private app sandbox, isolated from other installed apps. Network communications (such as external links and platform service calls) use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
