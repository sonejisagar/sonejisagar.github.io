Privacy Policy

Plain-Language Summary: Ring Lock: Number Puzzle saves your puzzle progress locally on your device without requiring an account or login. The App displays advertisements and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads + Support") handled through Google Play Billing and third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). We never sell your personal data.

1. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy explains how Sagar Soneji ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information in connection with the mobile and web game Ring Lock: Number Puzzle (Android package com.sagardev.ringlock.puzzle) ("the App").

2. Information Collected & Stored Locally

A. Gameplay Data Stored on Your Device

To save your progress without requiring an account or sign-in, the App stores the following non-personal data locally on your device (via Android SharedPreferences / browser local storage):

  • Campaign level progress, star ratings, unlocked levels, and Daily Puzzle completion dates.
  • 7-day reward streaks, weekly mission progress, streak freezes, and in-game coin balances.
  • App preferences such as sound effects, haptics, reduced-motion, and your opt-in daily reminder hour.
  • Local gameplay metrics and entitlement flags (such as ad frequency caps, session counts, and purchase unlock status).

B. Personal Data Not Collected Directly

We do not require user accounts and do not directly collect or access your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Ad Networks

Ring Lock: Number Puzzle includes advertisements (including optional Rewarded Ads for hints, continues, and double coins, as well as paced Interstitial Ads). To deliver and measure ads, integrated third-party advertising partners—specifically Google AdMob (Google LLC)—may automatically collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Device & Other Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) or app set ID.
  • Approximate Location: General region/country derived from IP address (never precise GPS).
  • App Interactions & Ad Telemetry: Ad impressions, views, clicks, and frequency-capping signals.
  • Diagnostics & Device Info: Device model, OS version, screen size, and crash/performance logs related to ad loading.

Purpose & User Controls: This data is used for serving relevant or non-personalized ads, enforcing ad frequency caps, verifying rewarded ad completions, preventing fraud/invalid traffic, and analytics. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Purchasing "Remove Ads + Support" disables forced interstitial ads while leaving optional rewarded ads strictly user-initiated.

For details on how Google processes advertising data, please review: Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers an optional one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase ("Remove Ads + Support") processed exclusively by Google Play Billing (Google LLC):

  • All payment methods, billing details, and receipts are handled securely by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card, bank account, or billing address details.
  • Google Play shares non-sensitive purchase confirmation data (such as order/purchase token and entitlement status) with the App solely to unlock and restore your ad-free status across app restarts or reinstalls.

5. Local Notifications (Optional)

After solving a Daily Puzzle, you may optionally opt in to a daily puzzle or streak-saving reminder (at most once per day) or toggle notifications off anytime in the Pause menu. Reminder schedules are stored locally on your device.

6. Other Third-Party Platform Services

Typography & Store Services: Google Fonts (Fraunces & DM Sans) and Google Play Services may receive standard HTTPS connection metadata (IP address and device user-agent) solely to deliver typography assets or platform updates.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Data processed through advertising or billing integrations is shared only with:

  • Google LLC (Google Play Billing & Google AdMob): For processing in-app purchases, delivering ads, fraud prevention, and aggregated measurement.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable law, subpoena, or valid governmental request.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored gameplay progress, streaks, and settings remain on your device until you clear the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App (which permanently deletes local data). Data collected by Google AdMob or Google Play Billing is retained and managed according to Google's retention policies. You may reset your Android Advertising ID at any time or contact us at the email below for privacy inquiries.

9. Children's Privacy

Ring Lock: Number Puzzle is intended for a general audience (ages 13+ / not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
